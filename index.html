Version History

Version 2.0.0
May 2026
  • Grouped inventory structure — products and stock lines are now separate
  • Same barcode scan now asks for expiry date and merges or creates new stock line
  • Cost Price, Selling Price, and Notes fields added to product form
  • Sales Log tab added for recording sales
  • App Mode selector on home screen (Shop coming soon)
  • Inline qty +/- controls on each stock line with Save and Cancel
  • Clean inventory cards showing only name, brand, status, and total qty
  • More Details expands to show full product info and stock lines
  • What's New modal on first open after update
  • Version History section added
Version 1.0.0
May 2026
  • Barcode scanning using phone camera
  • Product form with name, brand, category, description, qty, expiry, location
  • CSV export and import for backup and restore
  • Status system: Active, Out of Stock, Pulled Out
  • Record ID as unique identifier per item
  • Expiry tracking with 30, 90, 180 day filters
  • Home screen with summary stats
  • No Barcode option for items without a scannable barcode
