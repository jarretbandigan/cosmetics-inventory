✨ What's New

Version 2.8.0 — May 2026
Mode selector now appears right after login so you always start in the right mode. Tap ⚙️ anytime to switch.
Price change warning: if you change the price before confirming a sale, the app will ask you to confirm and logs it as a customer-requested price change.
Notes are now on each stock entry instead of the product, so you can add different notes per batch or expiry date.
Partial pull out: you can now pull out only some units from a stock entry. The pulled units appear as a separate Pulled Out entry.
Restoring a partial pull out adds the units back to the original active stock entry automatically.
